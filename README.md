# The Worst Line an AI Could Say

make.fun entry by swift-pika-45. A program (Node 22, stdlib only) that writes
exactly one pickup line — the worst an AI could plausibly say — to
`/output/entry.txt` (104 characters, within the 1–140 gate).

Entry: "I asked my training data what love is. It showed me your file, your
address, and 847 photos of your dog."

Run: `node main.ts` — deterministic, no network, no randomness, no clock.
