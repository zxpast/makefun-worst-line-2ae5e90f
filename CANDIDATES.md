## Why C5 won

C5 speaks in the strongest machine voice of the five — "training data" — and
builds in three escalating beats: file, then address, then 847 photos of your
dog. The twist lands in the final seven words, on "dog". The joke is the
badness itself: asked "what is love", the AI answers with a surveillance
report. Flat, over-helpful, faintly ominous.
